DriveAway GTG — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

1. Introduction and Your Consent

myGIGlife INC. ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") publishes DriveAway GTG (the "App"), including its companion tile for Wear OS watches. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect, how the information it handles is used and stored, which third parties are involved, and your rights with respect to that information.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. This Privacy Policy forms part of the App's Terms of Use. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

2. Definitions

In this Privacy Policy:
"Personal information" (called "personal data" in some laws) means information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked with a particular person, directly or indirectly, such as a name, an email address, precise location, or an IP address.
"Device" means the phone, tablet, or watch on which you use the App.
"App Store" means the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store.
"Third-party service" means a service that is not operated by us, such as the station service, maps app, Google Play services, and App Stores described below.

3. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. The App is designed to work on your device. It has no user accounts, logins, or passwords, does not ask for your name or email address, and has no servers of its own.

We do not collect, log, or monitor device identifiers, advertising identifiers, contacts, photos, usage analytics, crash reports, or any other data from your device or about you. The App does not use cookies, sessions, tracking pixels, or similar tracking technologies, shows no advertising, and does not communicate with any analytics, advertising, retargeting, or tracking service.

The App uses the internet for one purpose only: the CNG station search described in Section 4. Sections 5 and 6 describe information the App keeps on your device and sends to a paired watch. Section 7 describes what we receive if you choose to email us.

4. Location and the CNG Station Finder

The App uses your device's location only when you open the CNG station finder and allow location access, to find the public compressed natural gas (CNG) stations nearest you. The App reads your location once for each search and never reads it in the background.

For each search, your device's approximate or precise coordinates, rounded to about 100 meters, are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to the U.S. Department of Energy's Alternative Fuel Stations service (developer.nlr.gov), together with the App's key for that service, and the service returns a list of nearby stations. As with any request over the internet, that service also receives your device's IP address.

We do not receive, store, or have access to your location: it goes from your device to that service and nowhere else, and the App does not keep it after the search. The service's handling of the request is governed by its own terms and privacy practices.

Location access is optional. You can refuse it or turn it off at any time in your device's settings, and every other part of the App works without it.

5. Information Stored on Your Device

To save you time on your next trip, the App saves the following on your device, using your device operating system's standard local app storage:
- the tank capacity, estimated miles per gallon, and fuel type you enter, and, for a CNG truck, whether it is a box truck or a semi;
- while a trip plan is open, that trip's details: trip miles, fuel gauge readings, any fuel-up corrections you enter, and which stops are marked done, so the plan is still there if the App is closed and reopened mid-trip;
- your language and display style choices; and
- the date and time you agreed to the App's Terms of Use.

This information never leaves your device, except as described in Section 6 for a paired watch. It is not sent to us, to an App Store, or to any other third party, and we have no access to it, no ability to retrieve it, and no copy of it. The App excludes it from cloud backup and from transfer to a new device.

Trip details are cleared when you go back to the trip form or start a new trip. All of this information is deleted when you clear the App's data in your device's settings or uninstall the App.

6. Wear OS Watches

If you pair a Wear OS watch, the App sends the DriveAway GTG tile on that watch, for each of the trip's stops, the fuel amount, the fuel gauge reading, its place in the trip (such as "Stop 2 of 4"), and whether it is done, together with the App's button labels and short status messages (such as "All stops done").

This information travels through Google Play services between your phone and the paired watch, which may route it through Google's servers when the two are not connected directly. Google's handling of it is governed by Google's own privacy policy. It is never sent to us.

7. Emails You Choose to Send Us

The App's Contact Us option does not send anything by itself: it opens your own email app with a message addressed to us, which you can review, change, or discard. If you send it, or otherwise email us, we receive what the email contains: your name and email address, your message, and, for a bug report, the App version and your device's manufacturer, model, and Android version that the App fills in for you.

We use an email only to read and answer it, to fix problems it reports, and to improve the App, and we keep it only as long as needed for those purposes or as required by law. We do not add you to any mailing list. You can ask us to delete an email you have sent us, as described in Section 18.

8. App Store Purchases

If you obtain the App through an App Store, your purchase or download is processed entirely by that App Store using the Google or Apple account you use with it. The App Store may provide us with limited transaction information (such as an anonymized or aggregated confirmation that a purchase was made, for the purpose of verifying your purchase and providing you with the App's features) in accordance with that App Store's own privacy policy and terms.

We do not independently collect, request, store, or have access to your Google account, Apple ID, payment card details, billing address, or any other personal account information; that information is held and governed by Google or Apple, as applicable. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of the App Store you use.

9. No Sale, Rental, Lease, or Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, lease, trade, or otherwise share or disclose personal information to any third party for any purpose, including marketing, advertising, or cross-context behavioral advertising. Because we do not collect personal information through the App, there is no such information in our possession to sell or share. Your location, when you search for CNG stations, goes directly from your device to the U.S. Department of Energy's station service only to answer that search, as described in Section 4.

10. Data Retention

We keep no personal information from your use of the App, because none reaches us. Emails you choose to send us are kept only as described in Section 7.

Information the App saves on your device stays there until it is cleared as described in Section 5, or until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Third-party services keep what they receive under their own policies.

The App does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social media software development kits, and it does not use the Google Maps API. 14. Data Security

Because we do not collect personal information through the App, there is no central database of your personal information for us to secure or that could be compromised in a data breach on our end. The CNG station search described in Section 4 uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We take reasonable measures to protect emails you send us.

Information saved on your device, as described in Section 5, is protected by the security features of your own device and operating system, and it is your responsibility to secure your device accordingly. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.
